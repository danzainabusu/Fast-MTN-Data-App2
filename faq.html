Frequently Asked Questions

How do I fund my wallet?
Go to Wallet → Fund Wallet, transfer money to the displayed account and submit your payment reference. Your wallet will be credited after admin approval.
How long does wallet funding take?
Wallet funding is usually approved within a few minutes after payment has been verified by the admin.
Why did my data purchase fail?
Check that you have enough wallet balance, entered the correct phone number, and that the selected network is available.
How do I buy airtime?
Tap Airtime on the dashboard, enter the phone number and amount, then confirm your purchase.
How does Airtime to Cash work?
Submit your airtime details. After the admin confirms the transfer, your wallet will be credited based on the agreed percentage.
How do I contact support?
Open Help & Support to contact us through WhatsApp, Email, Phone Call, or submit a report.
